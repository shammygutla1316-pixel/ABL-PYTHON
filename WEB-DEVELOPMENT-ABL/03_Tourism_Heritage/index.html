Popular Destinations

FORT

Kondapalli Fort

A historic hill fort near Vijayawada, known for its views and history.

CAVES

Borra Caves

Natural limestone caves located in the scenic Eastern Ghats.

TEMPLE

Kanaka Durga Temple

A famous temple situated on Indrakeeladri Hill in Vijayawada.

Culture & Heritage

Andhra Pradesh is known for classical arts, festivals, handlooms, historic sites and traditional crafts.

  • Kuchipudi classical dance
  • Sankranti celebrations
  • Kalamkari art

Local Cuisine

Try pulihora, pesarattu, gongura dishes and traditional sweets during your visit.

Travel Tips

  1. Check local weather before travelling.
  2. Carry drinking water and identification.
  3. Respect local customs and keep places clean.
