Culture & Heritage

Andhra Pradesh is known for classical arts, festivals, handlooms and traditional crafts.

  • Kuchipudi dance
  • Sankranti celebrations
  • Kalamkari art

Local Cuisine

Try pulihora, pesarattu, gongura dishes and traditional sweets.
